Poster, genre, rating, episode count, release year and synopsis are pulled automatically from AniList. Add one at a time and pick the right match, or paste several names at once.

Separate names with commas. Each one takes the top AniList match automatically — no picking.

How placement works

  • Latest — the most recently added titles, newest first.
  • Top 10 — the 10 highest-rated titles across the whole library, ranked.
  • Popular — sorted by AniList's popularity score, highest first.
  • Movies — any title where AniList's format is "Movie" instead of "TV".
  • Hero spotlight — the highest-rated title released this year, poster and synopsis included.
